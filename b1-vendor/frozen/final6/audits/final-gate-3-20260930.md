- The user decisions in the "Update" sections of `audits/contracts-completion-gate-20260930.md`: UD-1 to UD-14 (section 6 below).

**Status rules:**
- **COVERED:** a normative clause states the behavior, and that clause names a conformance test id (`conf::…` or `bench::…`).
  - A generic verb (`create`, `update`, `delete`, `invoke`) on a Hub kind counts as tested by VB-3 to VB-6 plus the HC section 12 Hub CI rule ("every kind and action that a client hard-codes maps to at least one transcript").
  - A field or action with its **own** rule (a timer, a state machine, a filter, a refusal) needs its own named test.
  - A structural requirement (a library boundary, a crate boundary) counts when a named CI or suite check exists (C:449 section 11, HC:616 section 12, HP:652 section 19).
  - An absence requirement ("no residue of X") counts when a repo-wide search of the package finds X only in amendment logs.
- **PARTIAL:** the clause exists, but it has no named test, or another normative clause contradicts it, or a part of the requirement is missing.
- **GAP:** no clause.
- **USER-RULED-OUT:** the user (or the orchestrator under the user's delegation) removed or deferred it, and the package states that.
