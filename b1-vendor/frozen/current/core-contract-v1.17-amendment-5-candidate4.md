# Core contract v1.17, Amendment 5 — candidate 4 (real Core with injected edges replaces FakeCore)

The base files are the Core contract of `frozen/current`: v1.17, Amendments 2 to 4, and erratum 1. They are unchanged except where A5-5 names a replacement. Other contracts are cited by contract name and clause id only.

**Supersedes candidate 3** (`d34552cd…b69e`, commit `49f594f`), which the Amendment reviewer rejected (`verdicts/amendments.md`, 2026-10-01):
1. **Variations are produced at their source.** A5-2 has no decorator over `CoreApi`. Every seeded variation of section 11 comes from the testkit's injected **scheduler**, its **edges**, or Core's own **consumption boundary**. So coalescing is the real EV-6 rule, route frames are the worker's real framing, and nothing is buffered after real Core has already moved on.
2. **State and capacity stay exact.** A deferred operation has not progressed. Its slot stays reserved until the host polls its `Completed` (EV-5(a)). A transition and its event stay one atomic step (EV-5(b)). A due deadline is processed in its `pump` (TM-3).
3. **Two failure timings, kept apart.** A scripted **synchronous** refusal happens before admission and has no effect (ER-0, AM-4). An **asynchronous** failure comes only from an edge, through real Core's own completion path. No completion is invented.

Candidates 2 and 3 added the uncertain registry write (AD-7) and the worker named by role (erratum 1). Both are kept.

Author: the contract steward. Reviewer: the Amendment reviewer (Sol). Date: 2026-10-01. Decision: the orchestrator, under the user's instruction "if it's better long term, let's do that", adopted the hybrid of the steward, the Foundation lead and the Amendment reviewer.

**Why.** Section 11 requires a hand-written `FakeCore`. A second implementation of Core drifts from the first, and it cannot be faithful on terminal behavior without a terminal model, which steward rule R-7 forbids. This amendment keeps the section 11 **goal** ("A bug is fixed on one side without the other side's tests changing") and every **property** of the fake: seeded orders, failure injection per error code, and Hub tests with no worker process, no PTY and no sleeps. It gets them from **real Core with injected edges**.

## A5-2. Seeded variation, produced at its source

| # | Clause | Test |
|---|---|---|
| A5-2 | **`with_seed(n)`** sets the testkit's scheduler and edges. Each variation that section 11 lists is produced **where it arises in real Core**, never by reordering results that real Core has already produced:<br>- **event order across sessions, and completion order of concurrent operations** (OR-3): the scheduler chooses which ready work runs first in a `pump`. Each transition posts its event in the same atomic step (EV-5(b));<br>- **how many `pump` calls an operation takes** (OR-1, never zero): the scheduler defers the operation's progress to a later `pump`.
